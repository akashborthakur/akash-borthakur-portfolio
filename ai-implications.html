AI workflow lab / 01

Keep the human in the decision loop.

AI is most useful when it shortens the distance between messy inputs and a better first draft—without removing verification, context or ownership.

Where AI can help / 02

Use cases tied to real work.

Each use case has a clear input, a bounded AI task and a human-owned output.

Research synthesis

From notes to themes

Input: De-identified field notes, customer feedback and competitor observations.
AI assist: Group recurring themes, highlight contradictions and draft questions to validate.

Human check: trace each theme to source evidence.
GTM / content

From brief to first draft

Input: Approved audience, value proposition, proof points and channel constraints.
AI assist: Generate message variants, launch checklists and sales enablement drafts.

Human check: positioning, factual claims, tone and brand fit.
MIS / reporting

From variance to explanation

Input: Validated KPI definitions, targets, actuals and period context.
AI assist: Summarise variance, flag outliers and draft management commentary.

Human check: recalculate figures and confirm the denominator.
Field intelligence

From observation to next action

Input: Anonymised store observations, stock context and competitor signals.
AI assist: Categorise exception types and suggest questions or possible responses.

Human check: confirm on-ground context and assign an owner.
A reusable ChatGPT prompt / 03

Make the request structured.

Prompt quality improves when the model knows the decision, constraints and what counts as evidence.

Prompt template · market insightAct as a market-intelligence analyst. Using only the data supplied below, separate observed facts, possible explanations and missing evidence. Identify the three most important exceptions, explain why each matters, recommend a next action and measurable indicator, and state what a human must verify before acting. Do not invent numbers or fill data gaps with assumptions.

Input fieldsPeriod · geography · model / segment · actual vs target · stock / DOS · competitor observations · source / confidence.
Output contract

Useful, auditable and actionable

EvidenceWhat the supplied data literally shows
HypothesisWhat could explain it—clearly labelled
Next actionOwner, timing, KPI and verification step
Responsible operating rules

Automation can accelerate the work. Accountability stays human.

Do not put confidential customer, employee or commercial data into tools without approval. Treat generated output as a draft, check facts and calculations against source material, document material assumptions, and keep a human accountable for high-impact decisions.
