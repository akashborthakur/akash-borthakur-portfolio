Launch control calendar / 02

Use the launch window as a sequence of decisions.

Different milestones need different operating rhythms. A launch is not complete when bookings are captured.

30 Jul–10 AugCapture intent

Drive prebooking to the 2,100-unit goal; track branch and channel gaps.

From 11 AugConvert bookings

Track settlement daily; treat the 22% reading as an early checkpoint.

11–19 AugSuperweek push

Focus on unconverted bookings and close gaps at branch level.

After launchLearn & reallocate

Use sell-out, customer feedback and model/colour preference to adjust field focus.

What has to be ready for conversion?

The launch-control view balances demand generation with what a store can actually deliver.

AvailabilityVariant-wise supply, model stock, demo readiness and stock-out watch.
04
Conversion follow-upBooking owner, settlement status, open gap and next customer action.
05
Customer signalsDesign, colour, feature, price objection and feedback captured consistently.
06
Daily learning loopAchievement vs target, branch exceptions and corrective action owner.
What this demonstrates

Separate the leading indicator from the outcome.

Prebooking achievement tells us whether interest was captured. Settlement and sell-out tell us whether that interest converted. Reporting both prevents an early checkpoint from being mistaken for the final result.
