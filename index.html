01 / My point of view

Marketing is not
just messaging.

Great marketing connects an audience truth to a clear proposition — then gives the organisation a practical way to deliver on it. I care about both the story customers see and the operating system that makes it real.

01

Know who you’re talking to.

Segment with intent. Understand the buying context. Give each audience a reason to care rather than giving everyone the same pitch.

02

Make the proposition land.

Turn product features into a sharp value story, relevant content and a next step that fits the customer’s journey.

03

Design for execution.

Align channels, sales teams, stock and launch priorities. Track leading indicators and use the feedback to adjust the plan.

Play 02 / Consumer techLAUNCH GTM

Turn launch interest into launch momentum.

vivo Northeast · S2 5G launch planning

The launch challenge

Prebooking is an early signal — but launch momentum depends on converting interest into settlement, availability and sell-out.

The execution design

Track prebooking against target, identify branch-level conversion gaps, use field feedback and put focus behind the most time-sensitive actions.

1 Set booking and conversion milestones
2 Review achievement by branch
3 Surface settlement gaps quickly
4 Feed design and colour preference back in
Signals used

Outcome context: approximately 80% of S2 pre-bookings converted, compared with around 60% for earlier products in 2025 — an improvement of about 20 percentage points.

Play 03 / Channel GTMFIELD OPS

Make stock a sales decision — not just a number.

vivo Northeast · Distributor & retailer planning

The operational tension

Overstock ties up channel capacity; stock-outs slow sell-out. Allocation needs to reflect model movement and how much stock each partner can absorb.

The system

Use days of stock, model-wise demand, min-lot rules, coverage and retailer requirements to prioritise replenishment and reshuffling.

1 Compare stock with sell-out velocity
2 Prioritise distributors by DOS
3 Refill, re-shuffle or hold by model
4 Review coverage and stock health
