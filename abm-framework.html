Core building blocks

Designed to be practical.

01 / Select

Prioritise accounts using fit, intent, buying context and revenue potential.

  • Define ICP and account tiers
  • Map buying committees and decision-makers
  • Score accounts by fit, intent and readiness
02 / Align

Create one shared plan across marketing and sales, with clear account ownership.

  • Build account hypotheses and pain-point maps
  • Agree on plays, owners and success metrics
  • Coordinate sales enablement and outreach
03 / Activate

Deliver connected, role-specific experiences across channels.

  • Personalised content and landing experiences
  • LinkedIn and paid-media activation
  • Sales sequences and relevant proof points
04 / Measure & learn

Track progression at account level and refine plays based on evidence.

  • Engaged-account coverage
  • Meetings, opportunities and pipeline contribution
  • Play-level conversion and learning loop
Operating principle

Insight only matters when it changes the next move.

Use the framework as a living system: define the question, collect the right signals, align the team around action, and measure what changed. The details should adapt to the market; the discipline stays consistent.
