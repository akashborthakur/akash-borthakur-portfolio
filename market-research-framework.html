Insight-to-action logic

Don't stop at “what happened?”

SignalCompetitor promotion or weak model movement
→
InterpretCheck price band, stock and outlet coverage
→
DecidePricing response, supply shift or field focus

The decision follows validation. A single retailer comment or one-week spike is a hypothesis, not a conclusion.
