Core building blocks

Designed to be practical.

01 / Frame the question

Start with a decision to make, not data for its own sake.

  • Define business question and target segment
  • Set geography, channel and time window
  • Choose evidence needed to make the decision
02 / Gather signals

Combine primary inputs with reliable secondary and internal data.

  • Retailer and field observations
  • Competitor pricing, promotions and availability
  • Sell-in, sell-out, inventory and distribution data
03 / Analyse the gap

Turn raw inputs into patterns, comparisons and implications.

  • Share, price-band and model-level analysis
  • Coverage, stock depth and days-of-stock review
  • Identify whitespace, risk and opportunity
04 / Recommend & track

Translate insights into owners, actions and measurable outcomes.

  • Prioritised recommendations with rationale
  • Action tracker with owners and dates
  • Monitor leading indicators and update assumptions
Operating principle

Insight only matters when it changes the next move.

Use the framework as a living system: define the question, collect the right signals, align the team around action, and measure what changed. The details should adapt to the market; the discipline stays consistent.
