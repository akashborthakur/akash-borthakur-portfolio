Stock decision logic

Re-shuffle, refill or hold?

RE-SHUFFLE

Stock is available in the network, but sits in locations or variants with slower movement.

REFILL

Sell-out velocity and cover indicate risk of stock-out, and upstream availability is confirmed.

HOLD / REVIEW

Demand signal is weak, supply is constrained, or an assumption needs validating before moving units.

Exception-based field action / 03

Convert the dashboard into a route plan.

Use the exception—not the report—as the starting point for the next field action.

Exception 01High cover + slow sell-out

Validate model fit, display, pricing and outlet mix; check re-shuffle before sending fresh supply.

Exception 02Low cover + fast movement

Confirm replenishment, required variant and supply ETA; prioritise outlets that are closest to a stock-out.

Exception 03Coverage or data gap

Assign store verification, update stock evidence and rerun the decision with a reliable denominator.

The stock values are a historical example from a prior review. They are not presented as current live stock or current market performance.

Control-tower principle

Stock is a decision variable, not just a number.

A useful control tower links stock depth with sell-out velocity, outlet coverage, supply reality and an accountable next action. The review ends when the exception is resolved—not when the sheet is shared.
